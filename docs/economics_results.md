## Revenue model cross-validation

Model: log-linear ridge (lambda 30.0) on 126 Crustdata Company Search companies, rev-1 trained 2026-10-31. Features: `log_headcount`, `hc_growth_log`, `log_funding`, `funded`, `log_last_round`, `years_since_round`, `age_years`, `largest_country_us`, `largest_country_high_cost`, `hq_tier1_metro`, `cat_ai`, `cat_devtools`, `cat_fintech`, `cat_consumer`, `cat_b2b`, `log_size_bucket`.

| 5-fold CV (held-out) | Band hit rate | Median abs log10 err | Mean abs log10 err | Within 2x of midpoint |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Model (headcount-implied size bucket, as at inference) | 24% | 0.345 (x2.21) | 0.475 | 44% |
| Model (listed size bucket: reproduces Crustdata's band) | 25% | 0.329 (x2.13) | 0.414 | 45% |
| Naive baseline: headcount x median revenue/employee | 17% | 0.441 (x2.76) | 0.562 | 36% |

Target = Crustdata's own revenue band midpoint (interval-censored); the training set is 126 private companies from Company Search. Target is Crustdata's own third-party revenue band, not reported revenue. Search rows carry no web traffic, followers or role/region breakdown (not returned, or premium-billed), so those are not model features. Lambda and fit method were chosen by the same 5-fold CV that is reported (mildly optimistic). Crustdata's band tracks the listed employee_count_range bucket; 49 of 126 rows list a bucket other than the one their indexed headcount implies.
