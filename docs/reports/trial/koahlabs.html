10Methodology

How every number and status in this report was produced.

Pipeline

  1. Identity: the domain resolves to one Crustdata company ID (a tie abstains).
  2. Sources: the funding announcement and coverage are fetched; text addressed to AI agents is data, ignored.
  3. Claims C1, C2…: material Team & Financing statements quoted verbatim from those sources.
  4. Graph: founders' roles and prior employers joined by employer ID; rounds matched to the indexed record.
  5. Gate: code checks every amount, percentage, year and date against cited evidence; an entailment check confirms each sentence. Failures are withheld, never repaired.
  6. Signals and scoring: indexed growth, market and founder data feed five pillars and fixed flag rules.

Scoring model

Pillar weights: Team 25%Traction 30%Market 15%Financing 15%Verification 15%

Weighted score 89/100 → rating INVEST; confidence high. Suggested allocation = standard check × sizing ($100K × 100% = $100K).
